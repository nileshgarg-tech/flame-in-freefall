NASA Research Foundation
The Spacecraft Fire Safety Triad
Structuring 24 flight and ground investigations into NASA's core safety pillars
1. Prevention
Material flammability, Limiting Oxygen Index (LOI), and flame spread under forced airflow. Validated with BASS, BASS-II, Saffire-I/II/III, and CFI-G.
2. Detection
Microgravity aerosol agglomeration and smoke particle transport. Calibrates optical and ionization sensors using SAME, SAME-R, and DAFT flight data.
3. Suppression
Extinguishment kinetics, droplet extinction diameters, and gaseous diluent sizing (CO2 and Helium) based on FLEX and FLEX-2 experiments.

ISS & Orion Transit

Mars Base & EVA Suits
